Built around how you actually plan

Free-form blocks

Click any cell and type anything — there are no predefined categories or rigid event types to fight with.

Drag to merge

Resize a block by dragging its edge to span multiple consecutive time slots, just like merging cells in a spreadsheet.

Color-coding

Maintain your own color palette and assign a color to any block, so your week reads at a glance.
